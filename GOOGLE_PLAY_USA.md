# Google Play release preparation — OceanCentinell USA

Status: **DRAFT / NOT SUBMITTED**. Owner: Beacon of the Eagle LLC.

## Listing draft (English, US)
**App name:** OceanCentinell USA

**Short description:** Vessel, equipment, incident and maintenance records in one app.

**Full description:**
OceanCentinell USA helps maritime teams organize daily operational records in a single workspace. Maintain a vessel registry, document equipment condition, log incidents, and schedule maintenance tasks. View a summary of registered vessels, open incidents and overdue maintenance. Search records and export JSON backups and CSV reports.

This first version works with manually entered information stored locally on the device. It does not provide real-time vessel tracking, AIS/GPS telemetry, automatic safety alerts, network monitoring, or AI recommendations. It is not a navigation or emergency response system.
