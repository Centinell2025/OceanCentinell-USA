# OceanCentinell USA — Privacy Policy (prototype)

**Effective date:** November 22, 2026  
**Product operator:** Beacon of the Eagle LLC  
**Version covered:** local-first prototype v0.2.0

## Information handled
This prototype lets users manually enter vessel identifiers, last reported locations, equipment records, operational incident notes, and maintenance schedules. Information is stored locally in the browser or application WebView using localStorage.

## No account or remote collection in this build
The current app has no accounts, analytics, advertising SDKs, remote database, cloud synchronization, live GPS/AIS integration, or AI service. As implemented, manually entered records are not transmitted by the application to Beacon of the Eagle LLC. Hosting providers, browsers, operating systems and app distribution platforms may process ordinary technical metadata independently; this document is not a substitute for reviewing their actual services.

## Your choices
Users can edit and delete records, export all records as JSON, export individual tables as CSV, restore a previously exported JSON backup, or erase all local application records. Clearing app/browser storage can permanently remove local data.

## Data security and retention
Data remains until users remove it or the device/browser removes it. The prototype does **not** implement encryption at rest, user authentication or secure cross-device backup. Do not enter confidential, personal, regulated or safety-critical maritime records in this version.

## Children
This business-oriented prototype is not intended for children.

## Updates
This policy must be reviewed and revised before adding telemetry, remote services, AI, account login, analytics, crash reporting, ads or other SDKs. App store disclosures must accurately describe the **actual tested Android build**.

## Contact
A verified official business support email and publicly accessible policy URL must be added and reviewed by the publisher before submitting to Google Play. No contact address is asserted here without verification.
